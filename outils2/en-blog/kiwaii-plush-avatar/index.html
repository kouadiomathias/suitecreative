Pro tip Discord and small sizes

At 128px, fine details like thin stitching lines disappear. For Discord avatars specifically, increase the line thickness and go heavier on the button eyes. Bold features at small sizes are far more recognizable than delicate ones.

The single biggest factor in a great kawaii avatar is your starting photo.
