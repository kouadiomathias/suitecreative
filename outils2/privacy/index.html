Your images never leave your device

All creative tools on SuiteCreative process data entirely inside your browser. No image, file, or personal data is ever uploaded to any server. Privacy is not a feature — it is the foundation.

📖Introduction

SuiteCreative (hereinafter "we", "our", or "the platform") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our creative tools — including the video mockup generator, coloring page maker, sticker designer, and animal portrait generator.

Currently, none of our tools collect, store, or transmit your personal data or images. All processing occurs locally within your browser. No image is sent to any server, at any time, for any reason.

📊Automatically Collected Data

When you visit our site, our hosting provider may automatically collect standard technical information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and session duration. This data is used solely for aggregate statistical analysis and security purposes, and cannot be used to identify you personally.

We do not link any server-level logs to individual user identities or behaviours within our tools.

🍪Cookies & Similar Technologies

At this time, the site does not use advertising or tracking cookies. However, in the future, we may integrate advertising (for example via Google AdSense) to fund the service if traffic grows significantly. Should that happen:

  • Third-party advertising cookies would be used to serve relevant ads based on general browsing context.
  • You would be able to manage your cookie preferences via your browser settings or our consent banner.
  • This policy would be updated in advance to reflect any such changes.

We will always notify you clearly before any tracking mechanism is introduced.

📢Advertising & Third-Party Services

If we display advertising in the future, third-party ad networks (such as Google) may use cookies to personalise ads served to you. These providers operate under their own privacy policies. You can opt out of interest-based advertising at aboutads.info.

Our creative tools are fully self-contained. No API calls, no data sharing, no third-party integrations are currently active within the tools themselves. Zero data leaves your browser during tool usage.

🗄️Data Retention

Because our tools do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain. Any server-level visit statistics (access logs) are retained for a maximum of 30 days and then automatically and permanently deleted.

Images you work with in our tools exist only in your device's memory for the duration of your session. They are discarded the moment you close or refresh the page.

🛡️Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your connection. All traffic is encrypted via HTTPS / TLS. Your images remain strictly on your device and cannot be intercepted by third parties.

Because no data is transmitted to our servers during tool usage, our attack surface for privacy breaches related to your creative work is effectively zero.

⚖️Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable legislation, you have the right to access, rectify, and erase data relating to you. You also have the right to data portability and to object to certain processing activities.

As we do not process personal data through our tools, most requests would concern only server-level access logs (relevant if you have a static IP address). We will respond to any legitimate request within 30 days.

🔄Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect technical or legal developments. The date of the last update is always shown at the top of this page. We encourage you to review it periodically.

For significant changes — particularly any introduction of advertising or tracking — we will provide clear notice on the site before the change takes effect.

✉️Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle data, please reach out. We aim to respond to all enquiries within 48 hours.
