Frequently Asked Questions FAQ

Everything you need to know about T‑shirt video mockups

What is a T‑shirt video mockup and why do I need one? +
A T‑shirt video mockup is an animated presentation of your design on a virtual shirt — no need to print or photograph a real product. Videos generate up to 300% more engagement than static images on social media. For POD (print on demand) sellers, it’s essential: your Etsy, Amazon Merch, Redbubble or Shopify listings instantly stand out compared to plain photos. TikTok and Instagram Reels algorithms also heavily favor video content.
How do I create a free T‑shirt video mockup? +
It’s very simple with MockupStudio Pro: (1) Upload your T‑shirt image (preferably transparent PNG) by clicking the upload area or dragging and dropping. (2) Choose your animation mode among the 7 available (Wind, Floating, Wave, Mannequin, 3D Spin, Hanging, Stretch). (3) Adjust intensity, speed and turbulence to your liking. (4) Click “Export Video” and choose MP4 or WebM format. Everything runs in your browser — no registration needed.
What’s the difference between MP4 and WebM for my mockups? +
MP4 (H.264) is the universal format: compatible with all devices, social platforms (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube), Windows, macOS, and iOS. It’s the best choice for sharing. WebM (VP9) offers better compression at the same quality (smaller files) and is ideal for web integration. MockupStudio Pro automatically detects what your browser supports and offers the best codec available. Note: MP4 availability depends on your browser’s capabilities.
What image format should I use for the best render? +
For optimal results, use a PNG with transparent background (alpha channel). This allows the animation to see through empty fabric areas and looks much more realistic. The ideal resolution is between 800×1000 px and 2400×3000 px. Smaller images may appear slightly blurry. You can also use JPG or WebP, but the white background will then be visible. Free tools like remove.bg or Canva can easily remove backgrounds from your T‑shirt photos.
Can I use these mockups for my Etsy, Shopify or Amazon Merch stores? +
Yes, absolutely! Videos created with MockupStudio Pro are 100% free for commercial use. They are perfectly suited for Etsy product listings (product videos increase conversions by 60% on average), Shopify product pages, Amazon Merch and Redbubble listings, Instagram stories and TikTok shop. The 800×1000 px resolution matches Etsy’s video recommendations exactly. No watermark, no branding.
How long does the video export take? Is the video saved anywhere? +
Export usually takes between 5 and 20 seconds depending on the selected duration (2 to 12 seconds) and your computer’s performance. Everything happens locally in your browser: no data is sent to our servers, your T‑shirt image stays private. The video is automatically downloaded to your “Downloads” folder once recording finishes. You can create as many videos as you want, without any limit.
